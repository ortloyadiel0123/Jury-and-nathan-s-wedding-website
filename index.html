Our Story

How We Met

Juri's perspective: We had one class together during the Fall of 2022, but somehow, we went the entire semester without ever speaking or really noticing each other. It wasn’t until the very last day of class that our paths finally crossed. That day, we had to present our final project. When it was Nathan’s turn,and he was up there, he realized he had forgotten his password and couldn’t log in. As he struggled to recover his account and kept running into login errors, he looked up, seemingly lost and confused. We made eye contact for the very first time. We both smiled softly at each other. That was the moment that started it all. I offered to present first while he worked on getting his account and presentation ready. As I presented, he stared at me the entire time. When I finished, it was his turn, and I stared at him back with the same intensity. Once it was time to leave, he gathered his things quickly and rushed out of the classroom. I panicked and thought I would never see him again, so I rushed and followed, hoping he was waiting for me. And he was. From there, the rest is history. It’s still crazy to think that we spent an entire semester in the same classroom, never knowing that the person we were meant to find was sitting right there, until the very last day brought us together. Nathan's perspective: We had a class together in the Fall of 2022. There was a girl in the class who had caught my attention, not only because of her style, but because of the way she carried herself. She always seemed to know the answer, was kind and helpful to others, and simply had a good character. I thought nothing of it at the time. It wasn’t until the last day of class that I finally spoke to her. We were getting ready to present our final projects when I forgot my password and started getting nervous as I struggled to log in. At that point, I had pretty much accepted my fate. I looked up, and that’s when our eyes locked. She smiled, almost as if she was laughing at the situation, and offered to go in my place while I fixed the problem. After everything was sorted out and I finished presenting, I left the classroom because I was extremely hungry. When I walked out, I decided to wait for her. I figured that if she came after me, I would take the opportunity to get to know her. If she didn’t, then she would simply remain another person in the world. But she came running out of the classroom looking for me. From that moment, a friendship began, and that friendship eventually grew into the love we share today. What started with one class, a forgotten password, and a chance encounter became a lovely couple now waiting to be married.
